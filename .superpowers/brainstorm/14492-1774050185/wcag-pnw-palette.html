Colorblind Safety — Amber + Teal (Blue-Orange pair)

Blue-orange is the universally safe combination — distinguishable across protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia. Red-green pairs are avoided entirely. The amber tournaments and teal pick-up play labels stay distinct in all simulated vision modes.
